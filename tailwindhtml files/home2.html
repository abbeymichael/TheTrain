Three stops to boarding

A trip is simple to book, and built to actually help once you're there.

01 / CHOOSE YOUR STOP

Name what you're facing

Pick from divorce, loneliness, depression, grief, burnout, or anxiety — whatever fits, more than one if it applies. It's seen only by your specialist, never by other guests.

02 / RESERVE YOUR SEAT

Pay one fare

One price covers the trip, your stay, and your meals. Skip the food and the fare adjusts — flat or by percentage, shown before you pay.

03 / BOARD WITH SUPPORT

Meet your specialist

Each trip carries specialists assigned to specific challenges. You sit with people working through the same thing you are — guided, not left to figure it out alone.

One fare, no surprises

Your fare covers the seat, the stay, and the food — until you say otherwise.

Every trip is priced as a single fare that bundles your place on the trip, your Airbnb accommodation, and your meals for the whole stay. If you'd rather handle your own food, opt out at checkout — your fare adjusts automatically, either by a flat amount or a percentage set for that trip.
